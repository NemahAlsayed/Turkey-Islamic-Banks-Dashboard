Market Size

📊 Total Assets & Deposits Over Time. Tracks how Türkiye's Islamic banking sector has evolved by following Total Assets and Total Deposits across every available year. The selected year is highlighted in lime so you can compare it against the long-term trend and see whether the sector is expanding, stabilizing, or contracting.

Financing Portfolio

💼 Murabaha, Ijara, Mudaraba & Musharaka Financing. Compares the core Islamic financing instruments over time, revealing shifts in financing composition, customer demand, and the sector's exposure to different Sharia-compliant structures. Switch to "All Products" to see profit-and-loss-sharing instruments (Mudaraba & Musharaka) alongside the two dominant contracts.

Deposits

🏦 Deposit Structure for the Selected Year. A single-year bar chart breaking down deposits into Participation Accounts and Term Participation Accounts, or into Retail vs Corporate depending on the Customer Type filter. It's a snapshot of the bank's funding structure, helping you read liquidity sources and customer behavior.

📈 Click "Term Participation Accounts" to open an interactive drill-down showing the 1M / 3M / 6M / 12M maturity distribution — useful for assessing rollover behavior and liquidity risk.

Customer Comparison

👥 Retail vs Corporate Deposits. Compares Retail and Corporate deposits for the selected year, showing which customer segment contributes more to the funding base. Move the Year slicer to see how the balance between segments shifts over time — essential for understanding customer concentration.

Forecasting

🔮 Three-Year Linear Regression Forecast. Shows historical values for the selected deposit metric alongside a projection for the next three years. Actual values appear as a solid emerald line; projected values appear as a dashed lime line. Switch metrics below to forecast Participation Accounts, Special Current Accounts, or Term Participation Accounts.

Risk & Stability

⚠️ Asset Quality & Capital Strength. A dual-axis chart visualizing the Non-Performing Financing (NPF) Ratio, Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR) — the four core indicators of financial stability in Islamic banking. Use the Metrics selector to focus on a specific pair or view all four together.

ESG & Climate

🌱 Sustainability Performance. A combined bar-and-line chart pairing the Weighted ESG Score with a sustainability driver of your choice — Sustainable Sukuk issuance, GHG Emissions, or Social Impact Project financing. Highlights the sector's progress on green financing and ESG performance, increasingly important to investors and regulators.

Macroeconomics

📉 Macroeconomic Backdrop. A dual-axis chart tracking Inflation (CPI) against either Money Supply (M2) or PB Sectoral Financing. Provides the macroeconomic context behind Islamic banking performance, showing how liquidity conditions and inflation pressures move together over time.
